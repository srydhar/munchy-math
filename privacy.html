🐾 Munchy Math

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Munchy Math is a free math game for kids ages 4–12. This page explains, in plain language, what information the game handles and what it does not. It applies whether you play on CrazyGames, on our own site, or as an installed app.

The short version

Munchy Math has no login, no accounts, and no sign-up.

The only thing you type in is a nickname for a player profile (like "Player 1" or a first name) — this is never sent to us or anyone else. It stays on your device, or, if you play on CrazyGames, inside your own CrazyGames account so your progress can follow you across devices.

We don't run ads, analytics, or tracking scripts of our own.

We don't collect emails, real names, addresses, or any way to identify or contact a child.

We don't sell or share any data, because we don't have a server that receives it in the first place.

What the game stores, and where

Everything below is stored locally in your browser (or app), using standard "local storage" — the same technology any website uses to remember your settings. Nothing is transmitted to a Munchy Math server, because Munchy Math doesn't have one.

  • Player profiles — a nickname you choose (up to 14 characters), an avatar color, an age range, and a difficulty mode.
  • Game progress — scores, streaks, levels, which math facts have been mastered, unlocked costumes/collectibles, and daily-goal settings.
  • Preferences — chosen language and narration voice.

When Munchy Math is played on CrazyGames, this same information may also be saved to your CrazyGames account (using CrazyGames' own "Data" feature) so your progress can sync between devices. That sync is handled entirely by CrazyGames' platform, under CrazyGames' own Privacy Policy, which also covers any ads CrazyGames itself shows around the game.

Children's privacy

Munchy Math is designed for children, and we built it to need as little information as possible: no account, no email, no way to contact or identify a player. The nickname field is a free-text label chosen by the player or parent — we recommend not using a child's full real name. A parent can delete a profile and all of its data at any time from the in-game Parent section ("Reset progress"), or by clearing the browser's site data.

Third-party platforms

If you play Munchy Math on CrazyGames, the CrazyGames SDK is used for standard platform features (loading/gameplay events, optional ads, and the account sync described above). Those interactions are governed by CrazyGames' own Terms and Privacy Policy. If you installed Munchy Math from the Google Play Store, Google Play's own policies also apply to the app listing and install process — Munchy Math itself still collects nothing beyond what's described above.
