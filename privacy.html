Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Munchy Math ("the app") is a maths learning game for children. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Data We Collect

None. Munchy Math does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no login, and no connection to any server or third-party service.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves the following information locally on your device only, using your browser's built-in storage (localStorage):

  • Player profiles (name, avatar, chosen difficulty)
  • Game progress (score, streak, mastered facts)
  • Daily challenge streak and history
  • Voice and language preferences
  • Daily goal settings

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or any third party.

Children's Privacy

Munchy Math is designed for children aged 4–12. We take children's privacy seriously. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

Advertising

The app contains no advertisements.

Analytics

The app uses no analytics tools, tracking SDKs, or crash reporting services.

Permissions

The app may request access to your device's text-to-speech engine to read maths equations aloud. No audio is recorded or transmitted. This permission is entirely optional and can be disabled in the app's Parent settings.

Data Deletion

All locally stored data can be deleted at any time by tapping Parents → Reset progress inside the app, or by clearing the app's storage in your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
